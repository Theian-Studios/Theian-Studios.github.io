Snow Globe Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Snow Globe is a Mac app that shows falling snow on your desktop. It is made by Ian Sorensen.

No data collected

Snow Globe does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

What the app uses on your Mac

To let snow land on top of your windows and the Dock, Snow Globe reads the position and size of the windows on your screen and the position of your pointer. It does not read window titles or window contents, and this information is used only while the app is running to draw the snow. It never leaves your Mac and is not saved.

Your settings (such as how heavily it snows) are saved on your Mac only.

Children

Snow Globe collects no information from anyone, including children.
