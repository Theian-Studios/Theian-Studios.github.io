Snow Globe Support

Snow Globe lives in your menu bar. Click the ❄ at the top of your screen for all of its controls.

The snow on my Dock is too wide or too narrow

Open the ❄ menu → Dock Snow Width and pick the size that matches your Dock.

How do I get a snowman?

Snowmen build themselves when a window gets deep with snow — or choose Build a Snowman from the ❄ menu.

How do I stop the snow or quit?

Choose Stop the Snow or Quit Snow Globe from the ❄ menu.
