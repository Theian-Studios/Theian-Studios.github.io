Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 18, 2026

Loan Ranger ("the app") is developed by Ian Sorensen. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information We Collect

Loan Ranger does not collect, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information. The app has no accounts and no sign-in.

Loan data you enter (amounts, rates, terms) and any loans you save are stored locally on your device only and never leave it.

Advertising

Loan Ranger displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalized ads, including your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). When you first open the app, you will be shown Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and can choose whether to allow personalized ads.

If you decline tracking, AdMob will serve non-personalized ads instead. The app functions fully regardless of your choice.

For more information on how Google uses data in advertising, visit: policies.google.com/privacy

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used by Loan Ranger is Google AdMob for advertising. No analytics, crash reporting, or other data collection services are used. No personal data is shared with any third party beyond what AdMob requires to serve ads.

Data Retention

All app data (saved loans, preferences) is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

Loan Ranger is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any changes.
