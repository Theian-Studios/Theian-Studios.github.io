Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 18, 2026

Loan Ranger ("the app") is developed by Ian Sorensen. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information We Collect

Loan Ranger does not collect, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no network communication of any kind.

Loan data you enter (amounts, rates, terms) and any loans you save are stored locally on your device only and never leave it.

No Advertising

Loan Ranger does not display ads and does not use any advertising SDKs or tracking frameworks. No advertising identifiers are accessed or used.

Third-Party Services

Loan Ranger does not integrate any third-party analytics, crash reporting, or data collection services. No data is shared with any third party.

Data Retention

All data (saved loans, app preferences) is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

Loan Ranger is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any changes.
