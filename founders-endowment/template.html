Founders Endowment in AI and Data Science

The people who will shape artificial intelligence are learning right now. The Founders Endowment gives BYU students the chance to learn by doing — paid research with faculty mentors, aid that keeps them in school, and the computing power real work demands.

Founders who give today build something permanent: an endowment that keeps opening those doors for every class that follows.
